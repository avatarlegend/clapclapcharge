      Rules as built

      • Rounds 1 and 2 are clap clap charge for both players, so everyone has 2 mana from round 3.
      • Each round is clap, clap, then both players show a move at once. Whoever gets hit loses the game.
      • Reflect sends Hadouken and Shadouken back at the attacker. Anything bigger goes straight through.
      • Punch only beats Reflect. Any attack beats Punch. Against anything else nothing happens.
      • Tian Di goes through every defence, Reflect included.
      • Dig dodges everything above ground: Hadouken, Shadouken, Fireball and Tian. Earthquake hits diggers. The round after a Dig you're climbing out, so no Charge or Hypercharge.
      • Hypercharge gives +2 at once, then no Charge or Hypercharge for the rest of the game. A hypercharger who hits 0 mana loses straight away, unless their attack lands that same round.
      • Miss the beat and you stand still: no mana gained, and any attack hits you.
      • If neither player attacks, Charges or Hypercharges for 20 rounds in a row, the game is a draw.
